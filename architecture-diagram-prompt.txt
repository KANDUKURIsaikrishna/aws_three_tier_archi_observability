STYLE REQUIREMENTS:

  - Security, Identity & Compliance (IAM, Secrets Manager, ACM) = red (#DD344C)

- Arrows: thin solid dark gray/black lines with arrowheads showing direction of traffic/data flow. Use different line styles (solid vs dashed) to distinguish live traffic from async/replication flows.
- Include a small legend/key in one corner if multiple line styles are used.
- Overall composition: top-to-bottom or left-to-right flow, Internet/users at the top or left, data stores at the bottom or right.
